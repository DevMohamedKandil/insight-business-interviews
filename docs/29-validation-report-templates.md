## 1. Per-Interview Validation Report (Fill One of These After Every Interview)

```
# Validation Report — Interview #___
Date: __________

## Observation
(Only what objectively happened — no interpretation, no adjectives implying judgment.)


## Evidence
(Direct quotes. Measured behavior. Numbers — time, money, frequency. Nothing inferred.)


## Interpretation
(What the evidence above might suggest. Every sentence here must be traceable to a
specific line in Evidence — if it isn't, it doesn't belong here.)


## Confidence
[ ] High   [ ] Medium   [ ] Low
Why:


## Decision
[ ] Continue Validation
[ ] Measure More
[ ] Fix Critical Blocker
[ ] Reject Hypothesis
[ ] Validate Hypothesis

## Risks
(Only risks a specific piece of evidence above actually supports — not general worries.)


## Future Ideas
(Any product idea goes here, regardless of how good it sounds. Do not recommend building
anything from this section unless: (a) at least 3 independent participants have
requested it, or (b) it resolves a Critical Blocker preventing interviews from
completing. Otherwise it stays here.)


## Next Actions
```
